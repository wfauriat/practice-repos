## Quality Management

- **ISO 9001:2015**: Guadalajara manufacturing facility, certified since 2016. Scope: design, manufacture, calibration, and distribution of environmental monitoring sensors.
- **ISO 17025**: Meridian Calibration Laboratory (Boulder, CO). Accreditation number: CL-2847. Scope: temperature (−80°C to +300°C), humidity (10–95% RH), pressure (100–1200 hPa).

## Data Security (MeridianLink Cloud)

- **SOC 2 Type II**: Audit completed September 2023. Report available under NDA.
- **GDPR**: MeridianLink Cloud complies with GDPR for EU customers using the eu-west-1 region. Data Processing Agreement (DPA) available on request.
- **Data encryption**: TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest.
- **Data residency**: Customer data is stored exclusively in the selected AWS region. No cross-region replication unless explicitly enabled by the customer.

## Export Control

Meridian sensors are classified as EAR99 under US export regulations. No export license is required for shipment to most countries. Exceptions: embargoed countries per OFAC regulations.

## Warranty

All Meridian sensors carry a **3-year limited warranty** covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage or water damage (except MI-T100-W when used within IP68 specifications)
- Damage from chemical exposure (except MI-H300-CF when used within specifications)
- Calibration drift (calibration is a consumable service)
- Damage from firmware modification or unauthorized repair

Warranty claims are processed through Meridian's RMA portal at meridianinstruments.com/rma. Typical turnaround: 10 business days.
