# Meridian Instruments — Company Overview

## About

Meridian Instruments is a precision environmental monitoring company founded in 2011 by Dr. Elena Vasquez and Marcus Chen in Boulder, Colorado. The company specializes in industrial-grade sensors for temperature, humidity, pressure, and air quality monitoring.

## Product Lines

Meridian manufactures five core sensor models:

- **MI-T100**: Basic industrial temperature sensor (launched 2013)
- **MI-T200**: Advanced multi-point temperature sensor (launched 2016)
- **MI-H300**: Precision humidity sensor (launched 2017)
- **MI-P400**: Barometric and differential pressure sensor (launched 2019)
- **MI-AQ500**: Multi-gas air quality sensor (launched 2021)

All sensors communicate via the proprietary MeridianLink protocol and are managed through the MeridianLink software platform.
